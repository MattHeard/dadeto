# Noble’s House exterior

The perspective render made the flat silhouette and repetitive masonry obvious.
Minecraft’s [house guide](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/make-your-houses-better)
suggests overhangs and related stone textures for depth. The
[Raeyzeus guide](https://www.minecraft.net/nb-no/article/raeyzeus-top-5-building-tips)
suggests a coherent palette and concentrated details. Applied these to the
exterior and roof. The compiler rejects edits inside the prior room footprint
except solid-for-solid exterior masonry replacements. The v3 migration accepts
known v1/v2 block states. Inspect with the manor perspective render and verify
with `tabletop verify-walk`, `tabletop verify`, and `intuition verify`.
