# THREEFOLD Weftways caravan bypass

## Unexpected hurdle

The first focused evaluator found that the low quay was already intersecting Mara's body at the initial scale, so the growth-preview test did not demonstrate newly entered damage. The first geometry adjustment still left the quay below the enlarged body volume. The same first run also exceeded the reducer argument limit and reported three clones in scene copy helpers and legacy-save guards.

## Diagnosis path

The focused impact report isolated the reachable-choice, save, and renderer branches. Comparing `bodyBoundsAtScale` with the authored quay bounds showed the vertical overlap needed for a genuine growth-only intersection. The jscpd locations identified repeated bounds copies, creature copies, and legacy eligibility checks.

## Chosen fix

Moved the low quay's upper edge into the enlarged body's vertical bounds while keeping it above Mara's initial body. Routed scene bounds, creatures, and geometry lists through shared `scene-utils.js` copies. Reduced the flow-choice reducer to four parameters and injected route-specific controls. Added the Weftways route, renderer branch coverage, isolated schema-v1 save tests, and durable manual/harness/acceptance coverage.

## Next-time guidance

For scale-growth damage, compare authored physical volumes against the exact body bounds at both scales; a render preview alone does not prove a new intersection. Run focused lint, coverage, and clone detection early when parallel authored scenes repeat copy logic. The final local and exact-head CI checks remain the closure evidence for this loop.
