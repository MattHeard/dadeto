# Surface the Assembly's Final Seal plan in the handheld

- Unexpected hurdle: the campaign presentation contained detailed warning copy, but the only guaranteed handheld banner showed a generic tide label and did not present the Assembly's plan.
- Diagnosis: accessible presentation text was not enough for the pick-up-and-play flow; the compact canvas cue had to carry the plan and the physical cost.
- Fix: derive a warning cue from surviving Assembly representation and intact seal hardware, show three short 6px lines on 8px-aligned rows, and omit the plan in aftermath.
- Next time: trace player-facing fields through the actual canvas and page shell; a tested presentation object alone does not prove the player can see it.
