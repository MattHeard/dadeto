# THREEFOLD growth from a living occupant interior

- Unexpected hurdle: the growth preview used a newly-intersected-volume rule for both structures and the watchkeeper. A small Mara could enter the watchkeeper bounds, then grow while already overlapping them without a lethal preview or saved loss. The first local aggregate also detected an exact clone between the duplicated geometry and occupant preview traversals.
- Diagnosis: stationary structures should be damaged only when growth newly reaches them, but a living occupant remains vulnerable whenever the proposed grown body overlaps their volume. One renderer test that places Mara on the watchkeeper also needed to expect the intentional lethal highlight.
- Fix: share validation, next-scale calculation, ID normalization, and filtering in one helper with distinct impact predicates. Geometry retains the new-intersection rule; the occupant uses next-body overlap. Added an end-to-end regression that moves in from outside at scale −2, checks the warning, grows, and verifies the lost outcome through save/import.
- Next-time guidance: test volume semantics from both outside and inside an occupant; an intersection-difference rule alone does not characterize growth from within. Keep geometry damage and living-occupant consequences on separate predicates while sharing traversal code.
