# Survival Iron Man repulsor

The earlier repulse effect showed sparks only at the target. The Survival companion uses a villager model, so the shot starts at hand height with a lateral offset. Use stock electric sparks, end rod particles and sounds for the Switch through Geyser. The existing target and suit limits apply. A block ray trace from the shot origin rejects an obstructed shot before charge is spent.

In Survival, summon Iron Man, aim at a hostile mob within six blocks of him, and run `/ironman repulse`. Check the flash, streak, impact and knockback. Repeat from cover to check that an obstructed shot consumes no charge.
