# Process-new-story write batch effects

- Unexpected hurdle: `process-new-story` kept raw `WriteBatch.set`, `update`, and `commit` calls in shared core, and its simulator trigger bypassed the invocation boundary.
- Diagnosis: traced the Firestore trigger from `src/cloud/process-new-story/index.js` through core and the local simulator. Author `.get()` and page-number queries remain reads; batch construction and mutations were the unchecked write path. The first lint pass also caught parameter-bag arity growth and a 21-token clone.
- Fix: reused the permission-first batch facade from process-new-page; passed `AllowEffects` per write, update, and commit; bound the cloud and simulator trigger handlers at their invocation boundaries; staged batch injection to stay within the four-input limit; moved the stats write to eliminate the clone.
