# Threefold NPC and player palette separation

The cyan resident cap and role badges did not make NPCs easy enough to distinguish from Mara in handheld play. Keep the full resident sprite palette cool cyan, teal, blue, and ice; reserve warm gold and cream for Mara. Keep the palette test at rendered sprite-shape level so it covers actual pixels, not only marker metadata. The focused renderer suite can run with a workspace `TMPDIR` if shared `/tmp` is full.
