# Threefold small-world character silhouettes

- Unexpected hurdle: the player and residents used the detailed Mara/resident sprites in Broken Gate, but several compact locations reduced them to plain colored rectangles; body silhouettes alone still read too small in the phone-size preview.
- Diagnosis: `finishSmallWorldFrame` preserved actor color and position but did not carry role-specific body art into the finite-grid scenes. In Weftways, the Return Tide accord tile also painted over Pell at the same logical cell.
- Chosen fix: draw in-cell, 2px-grid pointed/flared player cues and flat-brim/straight NPC cues, then layer all characters above scenery. Physical body cells retain their authored scale and remain inside the logical actor cell. Add cyan `YOU` and magenta `NPC` tags, placed clear of actor bodies within the 160 × 144 field, because the small silhouettes alone were not sufficient at phone scale. A first oversized-portrait attempt broke containment and scale tests; a first minimum-size cue attempt flattened NPC width across scales. Both were narrowed to cell-bounded cues plus scale-aware bodies.
- Evidence: focused renderer, Grainlands, Hearthgaps, Weftways, and Silt Margin Jest passed 130/130. Chromium capture `/tmp/threefold-role-labels-phone.png` confirms the readable role tags and visible Pell at phone scale. `npm run build` passed. Final `DADETO_CHECK_PROGRESS_INTERVAL_MS=3000 npm run check` passed all 10 gates: 1,045 suites / 7,649 tests across six serial shards; zero clones and zero audit vulnerabilities.
- Next-time guidance: inspect every renderer path used by the reported scene. Verify exact draw order when an actor shares a logical cell with scenery, confirm the rendered screen at phone scale, and use explicit role tags when tiny pixel silhouettes alone are ambiguous.
