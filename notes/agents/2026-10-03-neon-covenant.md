keypad in its blog entry. Manual prose is lazy-loaded from the toy documentation.

The runtime now accepts episode create/step/journal systems. The presenter accepts
a runtime factory, agent registration adapter and export filename. Defaults retain
Mosslight behavior. Save profiles isolate identity and storage; lab imports rebind
trusted authored room geometry and sprites, and render-derived UI is recomputed.

The lab economy advances only through End shift: compute/cooling bottlenecks,
morale, data provenance, evaluation invalidation, payroll, recurring revenue,
deadline clawbacks, incidents and staff promises. No simulation service or real AI
training is contacted. The director has six decision points per shift.

Unexpected hurdles: the first strict scan exposed five new clones, including
controller selection and unrelated brace tails. Shared selection/application
policies and narrower state projection removed them; the baseline remains 73.
Browser failures were test assumptions: desktop touch controls are intentionally
hidden, so use keyboard there, and lazy manuals require the actual toggle button
rather than clicking its containing paragraph.

100% coverage across both games. `.tmp/neon-browser.log` verifies six phone/desktop
journeys including a fresh-save full campaign and embedded/standalone save sharing.

is `.tmp/neon-check.log`; broader shared-presenter browser acceptance is
`.tmp/neon-browser-regressions.log`. Terminal results belong in dadeto-srg7.
