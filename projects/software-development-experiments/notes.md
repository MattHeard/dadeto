# Software Development Experiments

## Outcome

Dadeto produces evidence about which software-development practices, tools, and workflows are worth keeping.

## Scope

Run explicit, bounded experiments involving agents, workflows, architecture, automation, interfaces, and the economics or ergonomics of building software.

## Success signal

Experiments produce evidence, not just novelty: a written hypothesis, an observable test, a result, and a decision about what to keep, change, or discard.

## Relationship to Dadeto

Experiments should improve Dadeto or produce transferable knowledge. They may temporarily tolerate uncertainty, but each experiment needs a clear stopping condition.
