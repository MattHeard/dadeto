# Personal Tools

## Outcome

Personal life and work are improved by tools that Dadeto turns from ideas into production quickly.

## Scope

Capture, prioritize, build, and operate tools whose primary user is Matt or whose primary purpose is improving his own effectiveness, understanding, or quality of life.

## Success signal

Dadeto regularly produces tools that are actually used, not merely technically completed.

## Relationship to Dadeto

Personal tools are the fastest feedback loop for the platform: they provide real needs, real users, and evidence about whether rapid delivery is valuable.
