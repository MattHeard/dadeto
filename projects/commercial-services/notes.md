# Commercial Services

## Outcome

Dadeto supports a commercial service with a paying customer and a repeatable delivery model.

## Scope

Identify service opportunities, test demand, package reusable capabilities, and operate paid or potentially paid offerings.

## Success signal

Dadeto supports at least one service with a clear customer, a repeatable delivery model, and evidence of willingness to pay.

## Relationship to Dadeto

Commercial work should emerge from demonstrated capabilities and real problems, not from speculative product-building detached from users.
