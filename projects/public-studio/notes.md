# Public Studio

## Outcome

Dadeto presents coherent topic packages that combine interactive toys and writing behind a single permalink.

## Scope

Publish useful explanations, show working tools, document experiments, and make the quality and direction of the work legible to other people.

## Success signal

Someone unfamiliar with Dadeto can understand what it is, see credible evidence of the work, and find writing or tools worth exploring.

## Relationship to Dadeto

The public studio turns private development into reusable reputation, feedback, and learning. Publication should follow real work whenever possible.
