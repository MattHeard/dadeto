## Problem Statement

The first playable slice establishes the Broken Gate; separate small-world routes apply the same scale-space controls to physically simulated water puzzles.

## Required spatial architecture — logical packing and semantic zoom

The live Rank 1 specification added this blocking requirement on 2026-10-22. Correct the logical grid/layout architecture and its automated acceptance tests before adding further gameplay content. Keep the 160×144 presentation and eight-pixel canvas grid, but treat them separately from the game's per-scale occupancy grid.

- **Canonical world and projection:** Keep stable identities, exact physical geometry, state, and causal history in one persistent world. Changing camera or view scale is a pure projection; it cannot move, damage, merge, kill, or otherwise mutate entities. Rendering zoom and physical growth/shrink are distinct. Only a committed physical action changes physical state.
- **Queryable logical geometry:** Every exposed actor, item, terrain feature, or composite has deterministic integer-cell bounds `(x, y, width, height)` at each requested scale. Bounds are separate from pixel positions and sprite dimensions. Positive-integer footprints may have arbitrary dimensions such as 2×2; they need not be powers of three.
- **Base-three mapping:** Adjacent scales change cell size by exactly 3×. Refinement splits one coarse cell into a 3×3 subgrid. Coarsening defines nearest-cell quantization, minimum footprint, stable ties, and origin/anchor rules. Projection round-trips may lose projected precision; canonical physical locations remain unchanged absent a game action.
- **Packing before rendering:** A pure deterministic layout over immutable world state returns visible identities, integer-cell rectangles, hierarchy/proxy data, and explicit occlusion, overflow, or unresolved-packing results. It accounts for proximity, containment, intentional overlap, stable ordering, and capacity. Distinct objects competing for a coarse cell must not be silently overdrawn, physically collided, destroyed, or have their canonical position changed; displayed cell placement may change only through the tested deterministic packing rule.
- **Authored composites and interactions:** Preserve persistent parent/child identities and authored scale thresholds. A coarse crate pile may be one interactable and reveal its crates at finer scale; a building may reveal walls, doors, windows, bricks, cracks, and marks with selected authored assets. ## Broader Small-World Milestone

The first Broken Gate release keeps floodwater as a deterministic visual consequence and does not run a fluid simulation. The broader small-world milestone begins with a pure hydraulic model: two rectangular chambers joined by one openable port. Chambers use floor elevation in metres, cross-sectional area in square metres, and water volume in cubic metres. An open port transfers water down the surface-height gradient using the Torricelli discharge relation, capped by the source water above the port sill and the volume required for hydrostatic equalization. The solver has no camera-scale input, so physical volume and flow are independent of the rendered view.

This bounded model assumes constant chamber cross-sections and a well-mixed water surface; it does not model waves, viscosity, turbulence, or arbitrary free-surface geometry.
