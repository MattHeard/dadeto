# THREEFOLD — The Weight of Water

## Summary

- Toy name: THREEFOLD — The Weight of Water
- Owner: Dadeto game workstream
- Last updated: 2026-10-20

## Problem Statement

Prototype a deterministic, scale-shifting handheld RPG in which the player changes the shared physical world instead of jumping between unrelated maps. The initial playable slice establishes the Broken Gate, canonical movement, scale changes, persistent rubble, and deliberate game-time advancement.

## Boundary

Compose pure simulation state with the existing Mosslight handheld renderer, browser input, and an injected save store. Keep browser storage and frame lifecycle outside the domain reducers.

## Scope

- Before growth, the frame previews intact geometry newly intersected by the next scale rung and the authored Latchfolk watchkeeper whenever the next body volume overlaps them, including when Mara is already inside their bounds at small size. The preview is read-only and never blocks growth. Geometry keeps its newly-intersected rule; occupant preview and committed loss share the next-body overlap rule. It predicts only this known occupant; other living-occupant uncertainty remains out of scope.
- The Broken Gate includes one physically authored 20.32 cm rat. When Mara fits wholly inside its bounds at scale −2, the frame previews that A grows through and kills it; X still bypasses. Otherwise, when Mara is within reach at scale −2 or −1, A fights and X bypasses; a fight repels the rat at −2 and kills it at −1, while bypass preserves it. The encounter fate and action cause survive complete-run save/export/import; schema-v1 runs migrate with the rat present and schema-v2 runs infer their prior cause.
- In scope: one contiguous gatehouse scene; an opening dock-worker warning and open-ended investigation objective; scales −2 through +1; directional movement; growth and shrinking; deterministic crisis stages; deliberate waits; slow guaranteed Anchor recovery at one charge per 15 in-game minutes; staged rescue of two abstract passenger groups at the ferry mooring, a Latchfolk warning in the lower settlement, and deliberate withdrawal by the upland road, with deterministic and independent outcomes; a first irreversible relief-gate branch whose damaged gate puts the lower drainage settlement at risk; a scale −2 locking-pawl repair before surge that restores gate geometry and keeps the lower channel dry while ferry and occupant outcomes continue independently; a late repair after ferry loss that restores the gate but lets water persist through surge and recede during aftermath without undoing losses; post-storm episode closure by filing a report through a surviving signal station or departing by the upland road when communication is unavailable; complete-run save, import, export, and reset; a 160 × 144 standalone handheld page.
- Out of scope: autonomous individual passenger/NPC schedules beyond the warning-triggered Latchfolk departure, general combat beyond the single rat encounter, other settlement evacuation interactions, branch-specific epilogues and campaign endings, physically simulated fluid dynamics, later campaign scenes, and scales outside the four authored bands.

## Actors and Interfaces

- Primary actor(s): Mara Venn; the dock worker has one opening callout, the physically bounded Latchfolk watchkeeper can be warned toward higher ground or lost if an enlargement overlaps their bounds, and the rat can be repelled, bypassed, or killed by fighting or growth based on Mara's size and choice.
- Inputs: arrows or directional pad, A to grow unless an in-reach rat makes A fight (except when Mara fits inside it, where A grows through and kills it), B to shrink, X to wait unless the in-reach rat makes X bypass, E or gamepad left shoulder or the touch interaction button to warn the watchkeeper, evacuate passengers at the ferry, withdraw through the upland road, repair the locking pawl at scale −2 before surge or after ferry loss, file a report through the intact signal station after aftermath, or depart by the upland road to close the episode; Y saves, and save utility controls remain available.
- Outputs: pixel-art frame, current scale/Anchor/crisis HUD, a normalized deterministic tide gauge, persistent geometry damage, independent passenger/ferry and Latchfolk outcomes, the single size-aware rat encounter with persistent fate and action cause, known-occupant and creature growth warnings, persistent withdrawal, pre-surge and late gate-repair outcomes, post-storm closure outcomes, the derived signal-station communication state, gate/drainage outcome and render-only surge floodwater, and versioned run saves.
- The shared Mosslight frame includes a deterministic screen-space tide gauge: fill follows elapsed crisis game time, capped at the authored aftermath boundary, and color follows the current crisis stage.

## Assumptions and Constraints

- Assumptions: the current Broken Gate geometry and scale-space reducers are canonical for this slice; each ferry interaction moves one abstract passenger group toward safety; warning the watchkeeper sends the Latchfolk toward higher ground; the watchkeeper bounds represent the one known occupant used by growth preview and resolution; and the rat's encounter radius is authored around its physical bounds.
- Constraints: gate, flood, signal-station, and communication outcomes derive from saved state and deterministic crisis time; geometry preview and committed damage use the same newly-intersected-volume rule, while the watchkeeper preview and committed loss use the same next-body-overlap rule so a small Mara inside their bounds is still warned before growth. The rat becomes actionable only at scale −2 or −1 and within its authored reach. If Mara fits wholly inside the rat at −2, A grows through it and kills it; otherwise, fighting at −2 repels it and fighting at −1 kills it. X bypasses an in-reach rat. Rat growth preview and committed fate use the same overlap rule. Fighting advances crisis time without Anchor recovery; bypass advances time as travel. Rat fate and action cause are stored in schema v3; schema-v1 runs restore with the rat present and schema-v2 runs migrate using the previous fight/bypass rules. Ferry rescue and the Latchfolk warning require an in-range interaction begun before surge; the first ferry interaction saves one of two groups and leaves the other aboard, while a second interaction saves both; at surge the ferry is lost, passengers still aboard are lost, a partially rescued outcome remains one safe and one lost group, warned Latchfolk are safe, and unwarned Latchfolk are lost. If the next growth body overlaps a still-present watchkeeper before that outcome resolves, the watchkeeper is recorded lost; safe or lost residents are not forecast again. After forcing the gate open, Mara can repair the locking pawl at scale −2 before surge to restore geometry and keep the lower channel dry through surge; the ferry, passengers, and Latchfolk still resolve independently. If it remains forced open through surge, the same repair after ferry loss restores geometry, leaves water through surge and lets it recede during aftermath, without reversing any losses. An in-range upland-road interaction before aftermath records withdrawal, advances the unattended crisis to aftermath, and closes by departure; after aftermath, an intact signal station can file a report, while the upland road remains a closure route if the station was destroyed. A closed episode prevents later world actions. Floodwater is a visible consequence, not collision geometry or a fluid simulation. The opening callout and investigation goal appear only before the first timed action; game time advances only from explicit simulation actions; waits after aftermath may restore Anchor but do not advance the crisis stage; rendering keeps Mara's sprite at handheld size; all new toy folders retain the four required docs.

## Dependencies

- Internal dependencies: THREEFOLD simulation, crisis clock, scene, renderer and save codec; Mosslight page presenter, pixel renderer and input adapters.
- External dependencies: browser canvas, localStorage when available, and the existing Dadeto build pipeline.
