# THREEFOLD — The Weight of Water

## Summary

- Toy name: THREEFOLD — The Weight of Water
- Owner: Dadeto game workstream
- Last updated: 2026-10-21

## Problem Statement

Prototype a deterministic, scale-shifting handheld RPG in which the player changes the shared physical world instead of jumping between unrelated maps. The first playable slice establishes the Broken Gate; separate small-world routes apply the same scale-space controls to physically simulated water puzzles.

## Boundary

Compose pure simulation state with the existing Mosslight handheld renderer, browser input, and an injected save store. Keep browser storage and frame lifecycle outside the domain reducers. The default `/threefold/` route remains the Broken Gate; `/threefold/?scene=sluice` selects the Tidal Archive, and `/threefold/?scene=silt-margin` selects the Silt Margin. Each region has a separate local save namespace.

## Broader Small-World Milestone

The first Broken Gate release keeps floodwater as a deterministic visual consequence and does not run a fluid simulation. The broader small-world milestone begins with a pure hydraulic model: two rectangular chambers joined by one openable port. Chambers use floor elevation in metres, cross-sectional area in square metres, and water volume in cubic metres. An open port transfers water down the surface-height gradient using the Torricelli discharge relation, capped by the source water above the port sill and the volume required for hydrostatic equalization. The solver has no camera-scale input, so physical volume and flow are independent of the rendered view.

This bounded model assumes constant chamber cross-sections and a well-mixed water surface; it does not model waves, viscosity, turbulence, or arbitrary free-surface geometry.

The first sluice-puzzle region now authors an upper reservoir and lower basin joined by a closed port. Open the service valve while Mara is in reach at scale −2; each deliberate action advances its physical water state, and a 15-minute wait raises the lower basin above the 0.3-metre crossing threshold. Grow to scale −1 to clear the crossing barrier. The route uses the shared Mosslight frame renderer, stores its state in a separate save namespace, and leaves the Broken Gate's render-only flood consequence unchanged.

The Silt Margin uses the same solver for a shallow dew channel, root basin, and scale −4 fine valve. The water spans the walkable crossing until its surface drops to 0.025 metres; explicit waiting transfers conserved water through the open port. Growing through the root arch and guardian offers a second crossing route with persistent geometry and social consequences.
