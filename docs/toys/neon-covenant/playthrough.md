# Neon Covenant controller playthroughs

These routes use only directions/A/B/X/Y. Reading, walking and inspecting never advance a shift. For all routes, select Ledger → End shift explicitly; the report appears afterward. B returns a report to the main menu, while X closes it. B closes a conversation; X then opens a fresh menu. Do not accidentally press X after B has already returned a report to the main menu.

## Independent local clinic model

From a new save, close or read the introduction. Open X → First-shift guide, decline the clinic advance for this route, then buy the 20k cooling repair. Exit the guide. Keep Atlas, standard size, local hosting, clinic-triage specialization and assisted oversight. Keep Ada and Jun researching, Sable evaluating and Ion in service.

Settle six research shifts. Atlas reaches its real 38-progress target; prototype and pilot milestones appear in the reports on the way. Open Research → Sable / test cases. Open each of the three cases and Run probe (2k each); use Other cases to move between them. All default Atlas cases pass at this checkpoint. They cost six testing capacity and three attention in total. Then reopen Research → Deploy. Training milestones alone are not deployment. Continue explicitly settling the remaining shifts, without more purchases or contracts. After shift 28 this tested route finishes independently with 198k cash against the inherited 120k debt.

The browser harness also exports this genuinely progressed campaign after deployment, converts its representation to the older rules-1 format, imports it through WebMCP, verifies unchanged finances/progress/people, and plays the remaining shifts to the same solvent resolution. No research or calendar values are injected to skip play.

## District-hosted clinic launch

Accept Mae's actual 28k clinic advance in the first-shift guide, then decline the physical cooling repair. Close the guide and open X → Research console → Configure program → Hosting → District pool. Inspect the 10k installation price, one attention and 6k recurring bill. Continue to the explicit confirmation and choose Apply setting.

This arrangement keeps four cooling units, halves local training demand and doubles yield per throughput. Keep the other Atlas settings and staff assignments at their defaults. Settle six research shifts, run the three representative Atlas probes for 6k total, then deploy. Settle the remaining shifts. The clinic is delivered before its deadline; the recurring hosting fee remains on every shift's report. This tested route finishes independently at shift 28 with 243k cash, 120k debt, all three Atlas training milestones, and no incidents.

These are current research/evaluation-checkpoint routes, not balance guarantees for future operating-deployment rules. Service adoption, negotiated contracts and other Release 2 systems are still being implemented. Update both fixtures and this guide when those rules change; do not preserve an obsolete route by bypassing its new requirements.
